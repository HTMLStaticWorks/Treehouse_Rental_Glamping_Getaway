Our Story

Reconnecting humanity with the heartbeat of nature.

Foundations

Born from a passion for the wild.

Arboria was founded in 2018 with a simple yet ambitious vision: to create a sanctuary where people could escape the digital noise and immerse themselves in the healing power of the forest without compromising on luxury.

Our founders, architects with a deep respect for ecology, designed each cabin to be a masterpiece of sustainable engineering. We use locally sourced materials and innovative techniques to ensure our presence in the forest is as light as a whisper.
