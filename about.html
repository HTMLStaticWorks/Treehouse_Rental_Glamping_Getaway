Our Story

Built by nature lovers, for nature lovers.

Reconnecting with the Earth

Treetops started with a simple idea: to provide a space where people can escape the noise of modern life and find peace in the canopy.

Founded in 2018, we began with a single treehouse built by hand using reclaimed timber. Today, we manage 12 unique properties, each designed to minimize environmental impact while maximizing comfort.
