Sustainable Luxury: How We Protect the Canopy

By Elara Vance • 10 min read • May 10, 2026

At Arboria, we believe that true luxury should never come at the expense of our planet. Our mission has always been to provide a high-end experience that exists in harmony with the ancient forest surrounding us.

Zero-Impact Architecture

Our treehouses are built using a specialized "suspended foundation" system. Instead of digging deep into the soil and disrupting root networks, our structures are anchored to the ground with minimal footprints, allowing water and nutrients to flow freely to the trees above.

"The goal was not to build a hotel in a forest, but to build a forest that happens to have a hotel." — Marcus Thorne, Lead Architect

We use reclaimed cedar and sustainable pine for all our construction. Every piece of wood is treated with organic oils rather than chemical varnishes, ensuring that no harmful runoff enters the ecosystem during rainfall.
