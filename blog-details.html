10 Ways to Minimize Your Footprint While Glamping

Glamping, or glamorous camping, offers the perfect middle ground between the ruggedness of traditional camping and the comfort of luxury travel. However, being in nature means we have a responsibility to protect it. Here are 10 ways you can ensure your stay at Treetops is as sustainable as possible...

1. Pack Reusable Containers

Avoid single-use plastics by bringing your own water bottles and food containers. We provide filtered water in all our treehouses, so you never need to buy bottled water.

2. Respect Local Wildlife

While it's tempting to feed the squirrels or birds, human food can be harmful to their health and disrupts their natural foraging behavior. Keep a safe distance and use your zoom lens for photos!

"In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks."

— John Muir

Sustainability isn't just about what we take away, but what we leave behind. By following these simple tips, you can enjoy a luxury experience while preserving the forest for future generations.
